REFERENCE DESIGN / PRELIMINARY TARGETS

Performance, simultaneous torque–speed capability, duty cycle, cooling, temperature cycling, environmental qualification, supply-chain origin, and safety functionality require validation. MIL-STD-810 ruggedization and U.S. manufacturing are program objectives. Slotless construction removes tooth-related cogging; energized torque ripple, system hysteresis, backdrivability, and pointing accuracy depend on the complete assembly and controls. Titanium corrosion resistance and thermal expansion compatibility must be assessed for the actual materials and environment.
