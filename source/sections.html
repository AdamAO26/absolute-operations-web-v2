01 / THE MOTOR PLATFORMIN DEVELOPMENT

Less mechanism.
More possibility.

Premium motors. Top-of-the-line integration.

We are developing a premium, ultra-shallow, slotless BLDC platform that couples the motor directly to the load. Mechanical packaging, windings, controls, sensing, thermal interfaces, and connectors are tailored to the application—from a compact optical payload to a precision robotic joint.

<20mm

Axial envelope target

A shallow architecture for space-constrained payloads and mechanisms.

Directdrive

No gear train in the motion path

Eliminating the gearbox removes its contribution to backlash and hysteresis.

SlotlessBLDC

Designed around smooth motion

A toothless stator architecture to eliminate tooth-related magnetic cogging.

Development targets are subject to prototype validation. System accuracy and energized torque ripple depend on feedback, controls, bearings, thermal conditions, and load integration.

Coupling the rotor directly to the load removes the gear reduction stage. Positioning performance will be evaluated at the complete assembly level, including feedback and control.

02 / ELECTROMAGNETICS

architecture.

Slotless winding development targets low-cogging motion for sensitive tracking and tactile applications. Torque output, ripple, and thermal limits will be characterized through testing.

03 / THERMAL DESIGN

path for heat.

Our proposed thermal stack explores boron-nitride-filled PCB encapsulation, expanded natural graphite interfaces, and a thin-wall titanium chassis. Materials and performance remain under evaluation.
